BrainRush — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026
Publisher: Zunozi
App: BrainRush (Android package: com.zunozi.brainrush)

This policy explains what information the BrainRush app handles and how. In short: BrainRush does not collect, send, or share your personal information. Your game progress stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

BrainRush does not collect any personal information. You do not need an account to play, and the app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal details.

The current version of the app does not send data from your device to us or to anyone else.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, BrainRush saves a small amount of game data locally on your device:

  • your high score
  • your total coins
  • your best answer streak
  • the highest level you have reached
  • your sound on/off setting

This data never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

If Android's backup feature is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this game data in your device backup. That backup is created and stored by Google under your device's backup settings; we do not receive it and cannot access it.

3. Third-party services

The current version of BrainRush does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-login, or in-app purchase services, and it does not share any data with third parties.

The app declares Android's standard internet permission, but the current version does not use it to send or receive any data.

BrainRush is distributed through Google Play. When you install or update the app, Google may collect information as described in Google's own privacy policy. That collection is done by Google, not by BrainRush.

If a future version adds advertising or any other service that handles data, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play data-safety information before releasing that version.

4. Children

BrainRush is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Security

Your game data stays on your device, where it is protected by Android's standard app storage protections. Because we do not collect or store your information on our side, there is no BrainRush account or server database containing your data.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. When we do, we will post the new version at this same address and update the effective date above.
